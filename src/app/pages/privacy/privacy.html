Our Commitment to Your Intellectual Property and Data Sovereignty.

Data Sovereignty

MayfairTech Expert Solutions operates on a principle of total client ownership. We do not provide infrastructure or hosting services; instead, we build the "Technology Backbone" directly into your private environment. This ensures you maintain 100% control over where your data lives and who can access it.

Intellectual Property Ownership

Unlike the SaaS "Rent" model, every line of code, database architecture, and mobile interface developed by MayfairTech Expert Solutions is a permanent asset owned entirely by the client upon completion. We do not claim rights to your proprietary logic or business IP.

Global Talent Governance

While we assemble elite engineering teams from across the globe, all confidentiality is managed through our Mauritius headquarters. Every engineer is bound by strict, project-specific Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

  • Strict module-based access to source code.
  • Legal accountability anchored in Mauritius.
  • Regular security audits of our standardized technology stack (Java, Angular, Native Mobile).

Open-Book Transparency

Our commitment to transparency is a core pillar of our strategic partnership model. We provide full visibility into the project governance and operational measures taken to safeguard your information throughout the development roadmap, ensuring an ethical and high-standard engagement from initiation to active development.
